ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Developing Cyber Talent from Education to Professional Practice

A specialized cybersecurity education and capacity-development platform at Nasser Centre for Science and Technology.

OUR PURPOSE

Practical cybersecurity capability for a changing digital world.

The Academy began operating with learners in 2023 following curriculum development, teacher preparation, cooperation with national cybersecurity stakeholders and the establishment of dedicated facilities.

Its model combines education, professional training, specialist laboratories, research, innovation, awareness and strategic collaboration.

What We Do

  • Structured cybersecurity education pathways
  • Professional and organisational training
  • Cyber range and specialist laboratories
  • Professional certification alignment
  • Research and innovation projects
  • National awareness and capacity-building initiatives
  • Academic and industry collaboration
OUR APPROACH

Learn. Practice. Certify. Innovate.

Learn

Build strong foundations in IT, networking, programming, cloud and cybersecurity.

Practice

Use labs, scenarios and professional tools to develop practical competence.

Certify

Connect learning to recognised professional and role-based pathways.

Innovate

Apply knowledge through projects, research, prototypes and competitions.
